CAMERA
------
W / S               Move camera forward / backward
A / D               Strafe camera left / right
Q / E               Move camera down / up
Right-click drag    Look around (mouse look)
Scroll wheel        Zoom in / out (changes FOV)
= / -               Zoom in / out (keyboard zoom, same as scroll)
O                   Toggle Orthographic / Perspective projection
R                   Reset camera + zoom to default overview

GAMEPLAY
--------
Space               Spawn ball at current hole tee
G                   Enter / exit AIM MODE (only when ball is at rest)

  AIM MODE:
    Left-click drag     Draw power line — drag backward from aim direction
                        (direction = opposite of drag, power = drag length)
    Release LMB         Fire the ball

[ / ]               Previous / next hole (for scouting)

TIME OF DAY
-----------
Numpad +            Advance time of day (faster cycling to night/dawn/dusk)
Numpad -            Reverse time of day
T                   Toggle auto time-passage on / off
                      Lamp posts activate automatically at night
                      (timeOfDay < 0.28 or > 0.72)

QUIT
----
Escape              Quit
